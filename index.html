JLPT N5 • Lesson 3

Introducing Yourself

わたしは プリティシュ です。

Watashi wa Puritishu desu.

Meaning: I am Prithwish.

The pattern is: わたしは ___ です。

JLPT N5 • Lesson 4

Particle は

は is pronounced "wa" when used as a particle.

Example:

わたしは インドじんです。

I am Indian.

AI • Level 1

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science concerned with building systems that can perform tasks that normally require aspects of human intelligence.

Examples include language understanding, image recognition, prediction and generation.

AI • Level 2

Machine Learning

Machine Learning allows computers to learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

Instead of manually writing every rule, we give the computer examples.

AI • Level 3

Neural Networks

Neural networks are computational models inspired loosely by the way biological neurons process information.

They contain connected units that transform input data through layers.

AI • Level 4

Deep Learning

Deep learning uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns.

It is widely used in computer vision, speech recognition and language systems.

AI • Level 5

Large Language Models

Large Language Models process sequences of tokens and learn statistical patterns in language.

Modern LLMs commonly use Transformer architectures.

AI • Level 6

AI Engineering

AI engineering combines programming, data, machine learning models, APIs and software engineering to build useful AI applications.

Example projects include AI tutors, document assistants, recommendation systems and intelligent automation.
